On this page, you will need to introduce your worklog. How did you complete it? How many weeks did you take to complete the program? What did you do in those weeks?

Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:
**Week 1:** [Onboarding, $200 AWS Credit, Skill Builder Premium, and GenAI Foundation](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Earning AWS GenAI Practitioner Certification, EC2 hands-on, and RDS Multi-AZ](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [AWS CLI automation, CloudWatch monitoring, and Route 53 Hybrid DNS](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Hybrid Storage, CloudFront CDN, VPC Peering, and Transit Gateway](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Systems Manager, Infrastructure as Code (CloudFormation), and Lambda automation](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [AWS Security Hub, KMS encryption, Docker containerization, and ECS CI/CD](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Amazon FSx, S3 Data Lake, DynamoDB NoSQL, and Savings Plans](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Serverless Big Data analytics (Glue/Athena) and application modernization](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [Event-driven messaging, Cognito authentication, and Advanced AWS AI services](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10:** [Kubernetes on Amazon EKS, AWS Fargate, and serverless frontend integration](1.10-week10/)

**Week 11:** [Serverless Document Management System, WebSocket APIs, and Elastic Beanstalk](1.11-week11/)

**Week 12:** [Threat detection, Amazon QuickSight BI, and Amazon SageMaker ML Capstone](1.12-week12/)
